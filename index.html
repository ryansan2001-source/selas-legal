Selas Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Selas (formerly Tend) is a calm routine tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. Short version: Selas has no account and no servers of its own, so we never collect, store, or see your data. Your routines live on your device, and (for Selas Plus users) sync through your own private iCloud.

Information we collect

None. Selas has no account system, no login, and includes no analytics or third-party tracking software. Selas has no server of its own, and makes no network connections to us. (Selas Plus's optional iCloud sync moves data only between your own devices and your private iCloud through Apple, never to us. See "iCloud sync" below.) We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Information that stays on your device

Everything you do in Selas is stored locally on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your routines and check-ins: the routines you create, your daily check-ins and skips, sub-steps, and your consistency history are stored on your device using Apple's on-device database. (Selas Plus users can also sync these through their own private iCloud (see "iCloud sync" below). Either way, this data never goes to us.)
  • Reminders: reminder times and the days you choose are stored on your device and scheduled as local notifications by iOS. They are not sent to a server.
  • Widget data: to show today's progress in the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Selas shares a small snapshot of your routines with its own widget through a private app group container on your device. This data never leaves your device.

Purchases

Selas offers a one-time in-app purchase, Selas Plus, that unlocks additional features. Payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; Selas never sees or stores your payment details. Whether you own the unlock is determined on your device through Apple's StoreKit, and that information is not transmitted to us.

iCloud sync (Selas Plus)

If you unlock Selas Plus, your routines, check-ins, sub-steps, and reminders sync across your devices using your own private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your personal iCloud database, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and iCloud terms. Selas has no server and no access to this data. We cannot see, collect, or share it; it moves only between your devices and your iCloud. Free users' data stays on-device and is not synced. You can turn syncing off at any time by disabling iCloud for Selas in the iOS Settings app, and deleting the app removes its local data from that device.

Calendar (Selas Plus, optional)

If you turn on Sync to Calendar (a Selas Plus feature, off by default), Selas adds your routine reminders to a dedicated "Selas" calendar it creates on your device. Selas only ever creates, updates, and removes its own events in that calendar. It never reads, changes, or transmits your other calendars or events, and none of this data goes to us. Those events sync through your own iCloud like the rest of your calendar. You can turn this off in Selas's settings, or revoke calendar access entirely in the iOS Settings app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Selas uses them only to send the gentle reminders you set up, and a single "focus complete" heads-up after a focus session. Notification content is generated on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Nudge (Selas Plus, on-device assistant)

Nudge is an optional assistant that helps you turn feeling stuck into one small next step. When you type a message to Nudge, it is processed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Your messages and Nudge's replies are never sent to us, to Apple, or to any server; nothing you type is stored off your device or used to train any model.

Health-related information

Selas is designed for adults with ADHD, so the routines you track may reflect personal or health-related habits. Anything you enter stays on your device, under your control, and is never transmitted to us.

Apple Health (Selas Plus, optional). If you turn on Sync to Health (off by default), Selas saves your completed Breathe sessions to the Health app as mindful minutes. That is the only thing it writes. Selas never reads any of your Health data, and nothing it writes to Health is sent to us. Health data is stored and protected by Apple on your device. You can turn this off in Selas's settings, or remove Selas's access anytime in the Health app.

Deleting your data

You can delete any routine inside the app at any time. Deleting the app from your device removes all of its stored data, including your routines, check-ins, and history.

Children

Selas is a general-purpose routine tracker and is not directed to children under 13.
