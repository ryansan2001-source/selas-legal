Do I need an account?

No. Selas never asks you to sign up or log in. Your routines live on your device.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Selas has no account and no server of its own, so your routines, check-ins, and history never come to us. If you unlock Selas Plus and turn on iCloud sync, that data also syncs privately between your own devices through your iCloud account (never through us). See the privacy policy for details.

What's free, and what's in Selas Plus?

Free forever: the daily check-in, reminders, the visual day timeline, mood check-ins, widgets, sub-steps, the full progress view, and up to three routines.

Selas Plus is a one-time $2.99 unlock, with no subscription, ever. It adds:

  • Unlimited routines
  • Focus sessions
  • Breathe, the one-minute guided reset
  • Nudge, a private on-device assistant
  • Capture by typing or voice, and "Pick what fits today"
  • "Just for today" tasks and a Someday list
  • The Apple Watch app for quick spoken notes
  • Multiple reminders per routine
  • Private iCloud sync across your devices
  • Optional calendar sync

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Open Settings inside Selas to enable notifications, and edit any routine to set reminder times and days. You can also manage Selas's notification permission anytime in the iOS Settings app under Notifications → Selas.

What is Focus?

Focus (part of Selas Plus) is a calm countdown to work alongside. While a session runs, Selas pauses its own reminders, and the time left shows on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island so you can check it without opening the app. You get one gentle heads-up when the time is up.

What is Nudge?

Nudge (part of Selas Plus) is a private assistant for the moments you feel stuck or overwhelmed. Ask it in plain words and it helps you take one small step. Nudge runs entirely on your iPhone, so nothing you type is sent to us or anyone else.

How do I capture a note?

On the Today screen, type into "Jot something down", or tap the microphone and speak (part of Selas Plus). Your words turn into text on your iPhone. New notes wait in the "Pick what fits today" card, where you send each one to Today or Someday. Nothing is ever marked overdue. Anything you don't sort moves to Someday the next day.

I don't see the microphone

Voice capture needs a language supported by Apple's on-device speech model. If you turned off microphone or speech recognition access, turn it back on in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security. The first time you use it, your iPhone may need a moment to download the speech model.

Can I capture without opening the app?

Yes, in a few ways:

  • Apple Watch: open Selas on your Watch, tap "Speak a note", and talk. The note appears on your iPhone in Today.
  • Siri: say "Add a note in Selas". Siri asks what the note is and saves it without opening the app.
  • Widget and controls: add the Quick Capture widget, or the "Capture a Note" control to Control Center, the Lock Screen, or the Action button. Each opens Today with the mic ready.

How do I get the Apple Watch app?

It comes with Selas. Open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps, and tap Install next to Selas. Spoken notes are part of Selas Plus; without it, the Watch app shows a short note explaining that.

Does Selas work on iPad and iPhone Duo?

Yes. On iPad, and on iPhone Duo when it's open, Today becomes a two-page planner: your day on one page and your list on the other. On iPhone it also works sideways.

Dark mode and text size

Selas follows your device's light or dark setting. To always use dark mode, turn on Always use dark mode in Selas's Settings. Selas also supports larger text, VoiceOver, Voice Control, and Reduce Motion.

Home Screen shortcuts

Press and hold the Selas icon on your Home Screen for quick access: start a timer, take a breath, add a task, or check in.

The widget isn't updating

Open Selas once so it can refresh today's snapshot, then remove and re-add the widget if needed. The widget rolls over to a new day automatically after midnight.

Selas Plus isn't unlocking on my other device

Open Settings inside Selas and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Your one-time unlock carries across all your devices at no extra cost.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual routines inside the app, or remove the app from your device to erase everything Selas has stored.
